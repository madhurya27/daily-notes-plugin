# Listing

The descriptions on the plugin's page in the
[Obsidian plugin directory](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/daily-notes-stream),
pasted there by hand (D-165). Git history shows how they changed.

## Short description

Read all your daily notes as one continuous, read-only journal timeline with
infinite scroll. Search every entry, jump to any date, and review past days on
desktop or mobile.

## Long description

Daily Notes Stream shows every daily note on one page, like a journal you scroll
through. There's nothing new to set up, since it uses the folder and date format
from your Daily notes or Periodic Notes settings. The newest day sits at the
top, and older days load as you scroll, so you can read back through years of
notes without the stream slowing down. Each note appears in full, just as in
reading view, with its images, tables, embeds, Bases, Dataview tables,
properties, and linked mentions. When you want to change something, click a date
to open that note, and the stream updates as you edit. To find your way around,
search every note at once or jump straight to any date. Sections you fold stay
folded, and empty notes that hold only your template, from Templates or
Templater, can be hidden. On phones and tablets, wide tables and Bases turn into
cards, so one swipe always moves the whole stream. It never changes your notes
or collects any data.
